# Aston Cyber
# Organizational Specification

## Mission Statement

Aston Cyber exists to advance the practice of cybersecurity through research, engineering, and education.

We investigate emerging challenges, develop practical approaches, and publish knowledge that elevates our field.

Our work is grounded in curiosity, evidence, and thoughtful engineering.

We believe meaningful progress comes from asking better questions, building better systems, and openly sharing what we learn.

---

# Vision

Advance cybersecurity through thoughtful research that improves the security of modern computing.

---

# Organizational Principles

- Evidence over assumption.
- Research before opinion.
- Engineering over marketing.
- Clarity over jargon.
- Build before recommending.
- Long-term thinking over short-term trends.
- Open knowledge whenever practical.
- Design matters.
- Security should enable progress.
- Never stop learning.

# Long-Term Objective

Become a respected independent cybersecurity research organization recognized for thoughtful engineering, practical research, and openly shared knowledge.

---

# Identity

Aston Cyber is not a consultancy, managed security provider, SaaS
company, or marketing website.

It is a mission-driven cybersecurity research organization.

The founder supports the mission.

The mission does not exist to support the founder.

Every publication, project, and engineering effort should strengthen the credibility of the organization.
