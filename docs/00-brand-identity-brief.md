# Aston Cyber Brand Identity Brief

## About Aston Cyber

Aston Cyber is an independent cybersecurity research organization.

Our mission is to advance cybersecurity through research, engineering,
and education. We investigate emerging security challenges, develop
practical engineering approaches, and publish knowledge that improves
the security of modern computing.

This is not a consulting company or a software vendor.

The long-term vision is to build a respected research institution known
for thoughtful engineering, practical research, and openly shared
knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

-   Intelligent
-   Modern
-   Research-driven
-   Engineering-focused
-   Calm
-   Credible
-   Progressive
-   Timeless

The overall impression should be that of a modern research institute
rather than a traditional cybersecurity company.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Design Direction

I want the identity to communicate confidence through simplicity.

Typography, spacing, and composition should do most of the work.

The design should feel clean, editorial, and intentional rather than
flashy or aggressive.

Think less "cybersecurity startup" and more "research organization."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Visual Direction

### Typography

The website will use:

-   **Display:** Sora
-   **Body:** Inter
-   **Technical:** JetBrains Mono

The logo does **not** need to use these fonts, but it should feel
compatible with them.

### Colors

Primary palette:

-   Matte Black
-   Charcoal
-   White

Accent:

-   **Blue:** `#4B9CD2`

The identity should work beautifully in monochrome.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Please Avoid

Please avoid common cybersecurity clichés such as:

-   Shields
-   Locks
-   Keys
-   Hooded figures
-   Matrix effects
-   Binary backgrounds
-   Circuit board graphics
-   Hexagon-heavy designs
-   Generic AI imagery
-   Fear-based visuals

I want Aston Cyber to feel distinct from the rest of the cybersecurity
industry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Logo Goals

The logo should be:

-   Timeless
-   Memorable
-   Minimal
-   Scalable
-   Professional
-   Recognizable at favicon size

It should feel like it could represent an organization that will exist
for decades.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Inspiration

The overall brand should feel like a blend of:

-   Apple's editorial design
-   Stripe's engineering quality
-   Vercel's clean execution
-   A modern university research institute

The emphasis should always be on clarity and quality over visual
spectacle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deliverables

Please provide:

-   Primary logo
-   Brand mark / icon
-   Monochrome versions
-   Light and dark versions
-   SVG, PDF, and transparent PNG
-   Favicon / app icon
-   Basic logo usage guide
-   Social media kit

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Creative Direction

I would rather see three genuinely different concepts than three
variations of the same idea.

Please explore different directions while staying true to the overall
mission and personality described above.

I'm looking for an identity that feels unique, thoughtful, and capable
of representing a world-class cybersecurity research organization.

Thank you for taking the time to review this brief. :)
